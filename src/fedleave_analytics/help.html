These are read-only planning tools. No action books leave or changes canonical leave records. Your original scheduled leave remains in the ledger. Export a plan to keep it; enter any real booking separately in Calendar/CLI using your normal workflow. A projected balance does not establish legal eligibility for a leave purpose.

The selected backend, exact datastore and leave year are authoritative. The year selector changes the initialized year being examined. File > Refresh reads the current backend again; editing controls normally reuses a cached snapshot. Missing stores are not initialized, and no alternate backend is silently substituted. File > Help opens this offline guide at the active tool. Hover over an action for a short explanation.

Summary reports totals and balances. Seasonality groups qualifying activity by month, weekday or actual pay period; choose its leave type and grouping. Calendar Heatmap shows activity by date. Overtime, Comp and Credit pages show earning/use/disposal activity and lot lifecycles. Their tables and charts explain the selected scope; these reports do not make a scenario.

What-if Planner: start here

  1. Open Calendar > Analysis > Planning > What-if Planner, or Analytics > Planning > What-if Planner. Check the leave year.
  2. Name the scenario. Set Cutoff (starting closing balance) and Horizon (last inclusive projection date). Both belong to the initialized year. New tentative use must be strictly after cutoff and on or before horizon.
  3. Choose a category. This selects the chart/results category and the default for new entries. Optional Reserve is a warning threshold for that category; it does not stop you entering a plan. Each category has its own balance and reserve.
  4. Use Add for extra leave, or Replace/Remove to choose existing bookings in a dialog. Edit the resulting draft row's category, hours and note as needed. Click its Date cell to open a calendar; click a day or choose Use date. Space/Enter on the Date cell also opens the calendar. Escape/Cancel leaves the date unchanged. F2 still permits ISO date text (YYYY-MM-DD).
  5. Edits recalculate automatically after a short pause. Recalculate runs immediately. Compare the unchanged Scheduled baseline and your scenario; inspect Summary, capacity and warnings before relying on the result.
  6. Export scenario JSON to save an editable plan. Save Table as CSV or Open Chart to share calculated results. There is no Apply to Ledger.

Add, Replace, Remove and Undo are different

ActionEffect in your scenarioEffect on original bookings
Add tentative useAdd an extra dated absence in addition to existing bookings.All existing bookings stay in the scenario and baseline.
Replace scheduled use...Open a list of effective future bookings. Check the ones to replace and confirm. The draft omits those originals and creates one editable replacement, initially with their combined hours and the first booking's date/category. Adjust it to your intended plan.Originals stay unchanged in the baseline and ledger. They are not counted a second time in the scenario.
Remove scheduled use...Choose bookings to omit from this scenario. A Remove operation has no new date/category/hours to enter.Originals stay unchanged in the baseline and ledger. This does not cancel real leave.

You do not need to select a row in the background table before using Replace or Remove. Their dialogs list date, category, hours, status, note and original transaction ID. Check boxes, then confirm; existing background selections are prechecked. Multiple selections are combined into one operation, even across dates/categories, so review its default date/category. Already-targeted originals are unavailable: edit or undo the earlier draft change first. Cancel adds no operation. If the list is empty, check cutoff/horizon or use Add for extra leave.

Example: 100 hours at cutoff plus 8 hours expected accrual minus a scheduled 16-hour absence gives a 92-hour baseline. Adding 24 extra hours gives 68; replacing the 16-hour booking with 24 gives 84; removing the booking gives 108. Undo the Replace/Remove operation to return to 92. A 24-hour entry on one eight-hour workday still produces a separate capacity conflict; the planner retains your requested hours to make the problem visible.

Read the results and validation

  • Scenario rows are your proposed operations. Existing scheduled leave is a read-only list of originals. Original transaction IDs and optional earned-lot links preserve provenance; they are not row numbers.
  • Dates may be weekends/holidays. Calendar selection establishes a valid date range, not usable capacity. Working-hour capacity uses configured workdays, tour/lunch and cached observed holidays; review conflicts and N/A context.
  • Summary compares cutoff, horizon, minimum balances, reserve/negative dates and shortfalls. A positive horizon balance can hide an earlier negative balance. Annual pre-cap/carryover/use-or-lose is shown only at the actual year end.
  • Daily closing balances share a date's closing total; they do not establish intraday order. Events and provenance distinguish source, synthetic and tentative entries. Expiring lots retain inclusive usage deadlines and explicit/FIFO links.
  • Red/error text in Validation means invalid input, such as an out-of-range date, invalid increment or stale original. Keep the row and correct it, or Undo it. Negative balances and capacity excess are valid but problematic results; they are not a booking approval.
  • Recorded Through Cutoff includes effective dated rows, including unresolved past planned/requested entries; it does not assert attendance. Historical cutoffs use present records, not reconstructed historical knowledge.

Saved plans and refreshed originals

Export scenario JSON saves the definition, source identity, assumptions and original fingerprints. Import asks Replace or Merge and checks a fresh read-only snapshot. Replace loads the saved definition; Merge combines operations and requires matching cutoff/horizon and no duplicate/overlapping targets. A rejected import leaves the active draft unchanged.

If an original changes materially after Refresh, its scenario row remains visible and invalid. Review the current booking, then select the affected scenario row and choose Resolve selected references to explicitly accept its current identity, or Undo that change. Resolution is not permission to change the ledger. Modified indicators compare with the last import/export. Plans are session-only unless exported.

Usage Forecast: historical estimates

  1. Choose annual, sick or combined annual/sick; set cutoff/horizon and optional category reserves.
  2. Review prior-year Reference and Exclude choices. Historical coverage starts unconfirmed. Confirm whole year only when the selected year's records are complete enough to treat missing activity as zero; otherwise select known-complete periods and Confirm selected periods. Revoke removes an assertion. These choices save analytics preferences outside leave records; they do not certify attendance.
  3. Calculate and compare Scheduled (source bookings), Typical (median history) and Higher (80th-percentile history). Higher is a planning scenario, not a probability or confidence interval. Missing history/exposure produces N/A, not a fake zero.
  4. Review monthly explanations for source years, rates, scheduled hours and additional estimated hours. Double-click a monthly row for source details. An estimate of 32 hours with 24 already scheduled adds 8; 48 already scheduled stays 48, with no additional estimate.
  5. Review incomplete allocation, daily balances, warnings and reserves. Estimated leave shares remaining work capacity, but unlike Leave Budget it is not capped by available bank balance: negative balances are diagnostic.

Current configured tours and cached holidays determine exposure when historical rosters are unavailable. Seasonal estimates need three eligible observations; whole-year fallback has its own coverage population. Run retrospective usage check compares estimates at earlier pay-period cutoffs against later recorded usage, using only earlier source years; it does not reconstruct bookings known at those earlier times. Export Results JSON retains the full calculation and check.

Leave Budget: protect eligible balances before deadlines

  1. Set cutoff/horizon within the initialized year. Explicitly check categories eligible for your intended purpose; none are assumed. Adjacent fields are optional bank reserve hours.
  2. Choose Even across pay periods (default), Earliest available, Latest available or Capacity-weighted. All prioritize earliest deadlines, then category, original earned date and lot ID. Even/weighted quotas use stable increment rounding and redistribute overflow; earliest dates fill each period's quota. Latest fills dates backward before the obligation's deadline.
  3. Read additional required hours separately from the informational unrounded required/usable-period average. Actual suggestions must fit dated funds, lot earning/expiry, purpose choices, reserves, increments and shared work capacity after bookings across all categories. The partial current period is labeled.
  4. Inspect deadline, credit-cap, period/date suggestions, daily capacity, cumulative, balance, audit and warning tabs. Double-click a row for exact details. Funding checks identify failing dates, candidate balances and reserve thresholds.
  5. Review after-suggestion risk. No additional use required, Feasible, Partially feasible, Infeasible and Unknown/unsupported are distinct. A partial plan stays visible; N/A is not zero risk.
  6. Optionally Send suggestions to What-if. A fresh source check rejects stale results. Existing drafts require Replace/Merge/Cancel; Replace reproduces the budget projection, while Merge retains existing operations and adopts budget assumptions/reserves, so its combined result can differ. Nothing is booked.

Annual required use is max(0, year-end pre-cap balance minus cap), after scheduled use/accruals/disposals: 288 with a 240 cap needs 48; another scheduled 16 yields 272 and 32 required, without double subtraction. Dated lots retain exact scheduled allocations and actual warn/expiration outcomes. Unknown opening provenance stays separate; past dispositions are not future opportunities; later deadlines are Outside current horizon. Midyear horizons do not claim year-end risk.

Two 16-hour lots sharing only 24 work hours leave eight unallocated. The cumulative maximum-flow check is a dated capacity upper bound; interacting reserves/caps are additionally validated on the actual schedule. A chosen-strategy shortfall does not prove every possible alternative is impossible. Supported credit caps replay sequentially once at actual period boundaries; this explicit planning-only assumption survives What-if transfer and does not alter default CLI accounting. Unsupported credit or payout policies report specific limitations while other categories remain usable.

Files, charts and troubleshooting

  • Refresh reads the selected backend again. Calculate/Recalculate uses its cached snapshot. Cancel stops waiting and discards late results; it does not undo draft operations. Use Undo/Reset for that.
  • Save Table as CSV exports the currently selected result table, its filters and assumptions. It is not an editable scenario definition. Use Export scenario JSON for a What-if definition, or Export Results JSON for full forecast results.
  • Open Chart opens exact supporting data with CSV, PNG, PDF and Print. Choose the intended category/table first. Exports must be outside the canonical datastore; the application guards against overwriting ledger files.
  • If no result appears, inspect status/Validation and confirm the year is initialized in the selected datastore. Missing holiday/schedule/lot context is N/A. Check configured time increments and cutoff < date <= horizon.
  • If a scheduled booking is missing from the chooser, it may be before/on cutoff, after horizon, ineffective/void, synthetic, an accrual or a non-absence event. If already targeted, edit/undo that draft operation before choosing it again.
  • If you close the application without exporting a draft, it is not saved automatically. Closing help or a chooser never changes a plan; canceling a date chooser preserves the original cell text.
